About Clade

Daily biology puzzles

Clade is a set of four daily puzzles about the animal kingdom. Every game has a new puzzle each day, the same for everyone, so you can compare results with friends. Daily puzzles are free.

The games

  • Kinship: find the mystery animal using the tree of life. Every guess tells you how long ago it shared an ancestor with the answer.
  • Silhouette: a blurry, zoomed-in shadow that sharpens with every wrong guess.
  • Vital Signs: guess the animal from its weight, lifespan and other life statistics.
  • Habitat: guess the animal from where in the world it lives.

The science

Divergence times, statistics and ranges are rounded estimates from open scientific databases. Full credits are on the in-game Credits page.

Who makes it

Clade is built by an independent developer, who also makes LEXICON, a daily word arcade. Ideas and bug reports are always welcome: lexiconarcade@gmail.com.

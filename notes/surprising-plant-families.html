Plant families with surprising members

Botanists group plants by flowers and ancestry, not by how we cook them. That puts some unexpected foods side by side. These families appear in Plant Families.

The rose family

Apples, pears, cherries, plums, peaches, almonds, strawberries and raspberries all belong to the rose family.

The nightshade family

Potatoes, tomatoes, aubergines (eggplants), chilli and bell peppers, and tobacco are all nightshades.

The cabbage family

Cabbage, broccoli, cauliflower, kale and Brussels sprouts are all varieties of a single species. Mustard, turnips and radishes are in the same family.

The grass family

Wheat, rice, maize (corn), barley, sugarcane and bamboo are all grasses. Grasses feed most of humanity.

The carrot family

Carrots, celery, parsley, coriander, dill and cumin are relatives, which is why their flowers all grow in umbrella-shaped clusters.

The gourd family

Pumpkins, cucumbers, courgettes (zucchini), melons and watermelons all belong to the gourd family.

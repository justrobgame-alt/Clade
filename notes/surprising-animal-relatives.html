Surprising animal relatives

Appearances can be misleading. Animals that live alike often look alike, even when they are distant relatives. Here are some surprising family ties, the kind you will meet in Kinship and Odd One Out.

Whales and hippos

The closest living relatives of whales and dolphins are hippos. Both belong to the even-toed hoofed mammals, the same group as cows, pigs and deer.

Elephants, manatees and hyraxes

The small, rabbit-sized hyrax and the sea-dwelling manatee are among the closest living relatives of elephants.

Birds and dinosaurs

Birds are not just related to dinosaurs: they are dinosaurs, the one surviving branch. A sparrow is more closely related to Tyrannosaurus than a crocodile is.

Horses and rhinos

Horses, rhinos and tapirs form their own group, the odd-toed hoofed mammals, separate from cows and deer.

Hyenas and cats

Hyenas look like dogs, but they belong to the cat-like branch of the carnivores, alongside cats, mongooses and civets.

Giant pandas and red pandas

The giant panda is a true bear. The red panda is not; it sits in a family of its own, closer to raccoons and weasels.

Fungi and animals

Mushrooms are more closely related to animals than to plants. Fungi and animals share a common ancestor that is not shared with plants.

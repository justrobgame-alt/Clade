Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what information Clade ("we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions? Email lexiconarcade@gmail.com.

1. Information saved on your own device

To remember your progress, Clade saves the following in your browser's local storage: today's puzzles and your answers, statistics and streaks, settings (such as light or dark mode) and a random player ID. This information stays on your device. You can erase it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

2. Advertising and cookies

Clade may show ads through Google AdSense to keep the daily puzzles free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at www.aboutads.info. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

3. Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

4. Children

Clade is a general-audience puzzle site and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

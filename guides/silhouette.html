How to play Silhouette

Silhouette shows a mystery animal as a shadow on a lightbox: zoomed in and blurred. Every wrong guess pulls the camera back and sharpens the shape.

The rules

  1. A blurry, zoomed-in silhouette hides today’s animal.
  2. Every wrong guess pulls the camera back and sharpens the shape.
  3. After two guesses you see its group, after four its first letter. You have six guesses.

Tips

  • Look for body parts. Ears, horns, fins, tails and the number of legs narrow things down fast.
  • Think about posture. Animals standing on two legs, coiled, or with wings spread are easy to group.
  • Use the group hint to decide between look-alikes.

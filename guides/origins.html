How to play Origins

Every crop on your plate was first farmed somewhere. Origins asks you to pin where wheat, chocolate, potatoes and other crops were first domesticated.

The rules

  1. Tap the map to drop a pin, then lock it in. Landing within 1,500 km counts as right.
  2. A route shows how far off you were, plus a fact about the crop.
  3. There are six crops and three lives.

An example

Potatoes were first farmed in the Andes of South America, wheat in the Fertile Crescent of the Middle East, and cacao, the source of chocolate, in the Americas.

Tips

  • Many crops come from a few centres: the Fertile Crescent, China, Mesoamerica, the Andes, West Africa and New Guinea.
  • Do not be fooled by modern farming. Where a crop grows most today is often far from where it began.

How to play Habitat

Habitat shows a map of where a mystery animal has been recorded in the wild. Your guesses appear on the same map, so you can see how their ranges compare.

The rules

  1. The map shows the mystery animal’s range in green. Your guesses appear in orange, and overlaps in purple.
  2. Each guess shows how much its range overlaps the answer’s, and unlocks a clue.
  3. You have six guesses.

Tips

  • Read the continents. Australia, Madagascar and South America each have animals found nowhere else.
  • Look at the edges. A range that follows coastlines suggests a seabird or marine animal; one that follows mountains suggests a specialist.
  • Compare overlap. A big purple area means you are close; adjust towards the green.

How to play Size Up

Size Up gives you a handful of animals and asks you to sort them by weight, lightest at the top and heaviest at the bottom.

The rules

  1. Move the animals into order with the arrows, then check your order.
  2. Animals in the right spot turn green.
  3. You have four tries. At the end, every animal goes on the ruler with its real weight.

Tips

  • Place the extremes first. The lightest and heaviest are usually obvious.
  • Use the green marks. Keep correct animals fixed and swap the others.

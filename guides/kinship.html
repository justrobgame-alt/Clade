How to play Kinship

Every animal alive today shares an ancestor with every other. Kinship asks you to find a mystery animal by measuring how closely related your guesses are to it.

The rules

  1. Type any animal. You see how long ago it shared an ancestor with the answer, and the branch of the tree of life they share.
  2. The hotter the emoji, from ice to fire, the closer the relative.
  3. There is no guess limit. If you are stuck, a hint reveals a deeper branch.

An example

Guess lion when the answer is the house cat, and you will see that they last shared an ancestor roughly 15 million years ago, in the cat family. Guess dog and the shared branch jumps back to the carnivorans, more than 50 million years ago.

Tips

  • Start broad. A first guess such as a human, a fish or an insect tells you which big branch you are on.
  • Then narrow down. Once you know it is a mammal, try one animal from each major group: rodents, carnivores, hoofed animals, primates.
  • Use the dates. A few million years means a close cousin; hundreds of millions means a different part of the animal kingdom.

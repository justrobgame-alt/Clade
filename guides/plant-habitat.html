How to play Plant Habitat

Plant Habitat is a botanist’s map. It shows where a mystery plant has been seen growing wild, and your guesses appear on the same map.

The rules

  1. The map shows wild sightings in green; garden and planted specimens are left out. Your guesses appear in orange.
  2. Each guess shows how much the ranges overlap and unlocks a clue.
  3. You have six guesses.

Tips

  • Climate is the biggest clue. Deserts, rainforests and cold northern forests each have their own plants.
  • Islands matter. Many plants grow wild only on particular islands.

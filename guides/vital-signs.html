How to play Vital Signs

Vital Signs is themed as a hospital monitor. Instead of a heartbeat, you see a mystery animal’s statistics: its weight, how long it lives, how long its pregnancy lasts and how many young it has.

The rules

  1. Two clue cards are open from the start; each guess unlocks another.
  2. Each guess compares its statistics with the answer: green means within 25%, yellow within two times, and arrows show higher or lower.
  3. You have six guesses.

Tips

  • Weight first. It separates mice from elephants immediately.
  • Long lives with few young usually mean large mammals, parrots or tortoises. Short lives with many young usually mean small rodents.
  • Follow the arrows rather than guessing at random.

How to play Twins

Twins shows two animals that look alike and asks a question that only one of them fits: which has ear flaps, which one hovers, which one is venomous.

The rules

  1. Two look-alike animals appear with one question.
  2. Tap the animal the question is about.
  3. There are six rounds and two lives.

An example

Alligator or crocodile: which shows its lower teeth when its mouth is closed? The crocodile. Seals or sea lions: which has visible ear flaps? The sea lion.

Tips

  • Learn the classic pairs: alligator and crocodile, seal and sea lion, frog and toad, moth and butterfly, rabbit and hare.
  • Read the question carefully. It usually points to one specific feature.

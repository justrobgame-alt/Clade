How to play Odd One Out

Odd One Out is a shelf of specimen jars. All of them but one belong to the same branch of the tree of life. Find the one that does not.

The rules

  1. Tap the jar that does not belong.
  2. After each round you see the branch the others share.
  3. There are six rounds and three lives.

An example

A whale, a cow, a hippo and a shark: the shark is the odd one out. Whales and hippos are close relatives, and both belong to the same group as cows, while sharks are fish.

Tips

  • Ignore looks. Body shape often comes from lifestyle, not ancestry. Dolphins look like fish; bats look like birds.
  • Think about the big groups: mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, fish, insects.
